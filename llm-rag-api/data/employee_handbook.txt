EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

VACATION POLICY

Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days per calendar year.

Employees should request vacation at least two weeks before the planned vacation.

Vacation requests must be approved by the employee's manager.

Unused vacation days can be carried over to the following year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

SICK LEAVE

Employees who are sick should notify their manager as soon as possible.

Employees may be asked to provide documentation for extended periods of absence.

WORKING HOURS

The standard working week is 40 hours.

Normal working hours are Monday to Friday.

Employees normally work between 08:00 and 17:00 with a one-hour lunch break.

REMOTE WORK

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Remote work must be agreed with the employee's manager.

Employees working remotely must remain available during normal working hours.

SECURITY

Employees must use strong passwords.

Passwords must never be shared with other employees.

Company systems must only be accessed using authorized accounts.

Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents.

TRAINING

Employees receive an annual training budget of 1000 EUR.

Training should be related to the employee's current role or professional development.

Training requests must be approved by the employee's manager.

EXPENSES

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days.

Employees must provide receipts for expenses.

Travel expenses related to business trips may be reimbursed according to company policy.
